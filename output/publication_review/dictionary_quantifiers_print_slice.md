# Editorial scope

These are short print-facing dictionary drafts for the quantifiers slice. They are aligned to `candidates_quantifiers.tsv`, interpreted through `dossier_quantifiers.md`, and cross-referenced to `grammar_quantifiers_print_slice.md`.

These entries are editorial review material only. They do not imply changes to analyzer dictionaries, machine dictionary files, or lexical source tables. This slice preserves candidate-backed examples and caveats rather than turning the topic into a raw search over every `khempeuh`, `peuhpeuh`, `khat`, `pawlkhat`, `kuamah`, `bangmah`, `tampi`, `tawm`, `zaw`, `mahmah`, or other quantifier-looking token. Review-note work for quantifiers has not yet begun.

## khempeuh

Category: universal quantifier

Gloss: `DET`; 'all'

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Universal quantifier `khempeuh`"*

Status: draft-ready

`Khempeuh` is the current candidate-backed universal anchor. The Genesis 2:1 example is a scoped noun phrase, not a license for raw `khempeuh` harvesting or generated-report counts. This entry is therefore enough for a cautious print dictionary draft, but it does not build a full universal/distributive system by itself.

(@dict:khempeuh-1)
a. Tedim: vantung leitung le a sunga omte khempeuh
b. Segmentation: van-tung | lei-tung | le | a | sung-a | om-te | khempeuh
c. Gloss: sky-on | land-on | and | 3SG | inside-3SG | exist-PL | all
d. Translation: 'the heavens and the earth, and all that was in them'

## pawlkhat

Category: partitive / existential quantifier

Gloss: 'some; one group'; with caveat

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Partitive / existential `pawlkhat`"*

Status: draft-ready with partitive caveat

`Pawlkhat` is useful, but the Genesis 32:8 example is partitive or alternative-grouping evidence rather than an uncomplicated bare `some`. The dictionary gloss should therefore keep `one group` / `one company` visible instead of flattening the row into a simple existential entry. The noisy opening `Pawlkhatah` token should not be substituted for the clean later `pawlkhat` control token.

(@dict:pawlkhat-1)
a. Tedim: pawlkhat
b. Segmentation: pawl-khat
c. Gloss: some-one
d. Translation: 'one group' / 'one company'

## khat

Category: numeral / indefinite boundary form

Gloss: 'one; a, an'; boundary only

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "`Khat` and the numeral/indefinite boundary"*

Status: usable only with boundary caution

`Mi khat` is reused from the numerals packet as boundary evidence. This entry keeps `khat` visible in the quantifiers slice without presenting it as an uncomplicated quantifier or article-like entry. The current draft therefore preserves the numeral/indefinite boundary explicitly and does not reopen numerals.

(@dict:khat-quant-1)
a. Tedim: mi khat
b. Segmentation: mi | khat
c. Gloss: person | one
d. Translation: 'a man' / 'one person'

## kuamah

Category: negative quantifier

Gloss: 'nobody; no one'

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Negative quantifiers and negation overlap"*

Status: draft-ready with negation-overlap caveat

`Kuamah` is usable here only in a negative-licensed clause. The accepted example `kuamah mu lo` is controlled quantifier evidence, but it should cross-reference rather than reopen the negation packet. Raw `kuamah` hits should not be treated as print evidence unless the clause is checked for negative licensing and negation overlap.

(@dict:kuamah-1)
a. Tedim: kuamah mu lo
b. Segmentation: kuamah | mu | lo
c. Gloss: nobody | see.I | NEG
d. Translation: 'he saw nobody' / 'he saw no man'

## bangmah

Category: negative quantifier / bang-family overlap form

Gloss: 'nothing'; only in negative-licensed contexts in this slice

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Negative quantifiers and negation overlap"; "Bang-family false friends"*

Status: draft-ready with negative-licensing and bang-family caveats

`Bangmah` is usable as quantifier evidence only in the accepted negative-licensed row `bangmah om lo hi`. It also has bang-family and interrogative-overlap risks, so the present dictionary slice must not promote `bangmah` outside clear negative licensing. The blocked control `tua bangmah hi-in` remains blocked and should not be treated as ordinary quantifier evidence.

(@dict:bangmah-1)
a. Tedim: bangmah om lo hi
b. Segmentation: bangmah | om | lo | hi
c. Gloss: nothing | exist | NEG | DECL
d. Translation: 'there is nothing' / 'nothing exists'

Blocked control note: `tua bangmah hi-in` is blocked as ordinary quantifier evidence.

## Deferred and blocked material

This dictionary slice stays deliberately narrow. `Peuhpeuh` remains deferred and not print-ready. `Tawm` remains deferred and not print-ready. `Tua bangmah hi-in` remains blocked. `Khat` remains boundary evidence and should not be absorbed from numerals as a simple article-like quantifier. Raw generated-report frequency tables remain excluded. Broad degree/intensifier/comparative prose remains deferred. Coordinators and sentence-final particles remain out of scope.
