# Scope and status

This dossier defines a first candidate-first scope for basic Tedim verb paradigms. The target is a narrow finite-predicate slice, not a full verbal morphology chapter.

The scope is intentionally constrained to finite-frame anchors and person-marked finite predicates that remain clear after cross-checking with existing prefix/agreement, TAM, stem-alternation, transitivity, and hong/kong sections.

# Person-configuration and finite-frame diagnostic summary

## Promoted finite-frame anchors

- `ka-nei hi` (Genesis 21:7) is promoted because it cleanly shows a finite predicate frame with person-marked predicate plus clause-final `hi`.
- `a en uh hi` (Matthew 27:36) is promoted because it provides a Gospel finite frame with overt predicate and plural `uh`.

## Promoted person-marking anchors

- `na si ding hi` (Genesis 2:17) is promoted as the clearest second-person finite predicate frame in the current candidate layer.
- `a suak hi` (Genesis 26:13) is promoted as a compact third-person finite predicate frame.
- `ka-nei kei hi` (John 4:17) is promoted with caveat as a Gospel first-person finite predicate frame.

## Supporting paradigm rows

- `nei / neih` is kept as supporting evidence because it links finite and derived environments without forcing a full stem paradigm.

# Boundary categories

## Negation boundary rows

- `ta nei lo hi` is kept as `negation_boundary`: finite framing is visible, but negation interpretation belongs to the negation section.

## TAM boundary rows

- `lutthei ding uh hi` is kept as `tam_boundary`: finite framing is present, but abilitative+irrealis stacking belongs to TAM analysis.

## Stem-alternation boundary rows

- `mu` and `muh` are kept as `stem_alternation_boundary` rows because the finite paradigm slice should not reopen the Form I/Form II system.

## Object-prefix boundary rows

- `hongbia` and `kongkoih` are retained as `object_prefix_boundary` rows to show adjacency, but they remain owned by the hong/kong section.

## Analyzer-gap and blocked rows

- report-table `ipai` stays blocked (`analyzer_gap_blocked`);
- conditioned NEG.ABIL variants `-pah/-pak/-lawh` stay blocked (`analyzer_gap_blocked`).

## Report-only rows not yet promoted

- report-table `ka pai` with artifact segmentation (`kap-ai`) stays deferred as `lexicalized_or_unclear` and is not promoted to grammar prose.

# Decision

The first verb-paradigm slice should be framed around finite predicate structure and person-marked finite anchors only. It should keep TAM, negation, stem alternation, object-prefix, and analyzer-gap domains explicit as boundaries.

This scope supports a controlled grammar-facing section while avoiding premature claims about a complete Tedim verbal paradigm system.
