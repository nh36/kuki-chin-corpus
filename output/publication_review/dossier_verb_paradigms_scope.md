# Report-alignment diagnostic summary

A dedicated alignment diagnostic now lives at:

- `output/publication_review/verb_paradigm_report_alignment_diagnostic.md`

Key stabilization decisions from that diagnostic:

- The paradigm-table reports are treated as discovery surfaces, not automatically print-safe grammar evidence.
- The current section remains under `Verb paradigms`, but its safe claim is finite-predicate-frame plus person-marked anchors, not a complete paradigm system.
- Surface orthography and segmented analysis are now explicitly separated (`ka nei hi` in prose/inventory vs `ka-nei` in segmentation/parse fields).
- Report-table-only rows (`i pai`, artifact-prone `ka pai`) remain non-promoted.

# Promoted finite-frame anchors

- `ka nei hi` (Genesis 21:7) remains promoted because it cleanly shows a finite predicate frame with person-marked predicate plus clause-final `hi`.
- `a en uh hi` (Matthew 27:36) remains promoted with caveat because it provides a Gospel finite frame with overt predicate and plural `uh`.

# Promoted person-marking anchors

- `na si ding hi` (Genesis 2:17) remains promoted as the clearest second-person finite predicate frame in the current candidate layer.
- `a suak hi` (Genesis 26:13) remains promoted as a compact third-person finite predicate frame.
- `ka nei kei hi` (John 4:17) remains promoted with caveat as a Gospel first-person finite predicate frame with negation overlap.

# Supporting paradigm rows

- `nei / neih` remains supporting evidence because it links finite and derived environments without forcing a full stem paradigm.

# Boundary categories

## Negation boundary rows

- `ta nei lo hi` is kept as `negation_boundary`: finite framing is visible, but negation interpretation belongs to the negation section.

## TAM boundary rows

- `lutthei ding uh hi` is kept as `tam_boundary`: finite framing is present, but abilitative+irrealis stacking belongs to TAM analysis.

## Stem-alternation boundary rows

- `mu` and `muh` are kept as `stem_alternation_boundary` rows because the finite paradigm slice should not reopen the Form I/Form II system.

## Object-prefix boundary rows

- `hong bia` and `kong koih` are retained as `object_prefix_boundary` rows to show adjacency, but they remain owned by the hong/kong section.

## Analyzer-gap and blocked rows

- conditioned NEG.ABIL variants `-pah/-pak/-lawh` stay blocked (`analyzer_gap_blocked`).

## Report-only rows not yet promoted

- report-table `i pai` stays blocked as `report_table_only`;
- report-table `ka pai` with artifact segmentation (`kap-ai`) stays deferred as `lexicalized_or_unclear`;
- both remain outside grammar-facing promotion.

# Decision

The current section is stabilized as a finite-predicate-frame/person-marked first slice within the broader verb-paradigm domain.

This scope keeps claims accurate, preserves report-table artifacts as blocked/deferred, and leaves fuller paradigm treatment for later dedicated passes.
