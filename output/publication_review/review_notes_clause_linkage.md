# Why there is no dictionary slice

There is no dictionary slice because this packet is constructional/clausal rather than lexical. It should not create dictionary entries for `ciangin` or `dingin`.

The current packet is about temporal subordination and clause-linkage boundaries, not about whether `ciangin` and `dingin` should be exported as lexical headwords. That is why the packet is complete with review notes and without a dictionary layer.

# What does not yet work

This packet does not provide a full complex-sentence chapter. It does not provide a full switch-reference chapter. It does not provide a full relative-clause chapter. It does not provide a full subordination inventory. It does not provide a full account of nominalized clauses.

It also does not yet provide a settled account of same-subject chaining, different-subject linkage, relative-clause formation, or nominalization-plus-case routing. That restraint is useful. The packet works because it keeps the first claim smaller than the unresolved architecture around it.
