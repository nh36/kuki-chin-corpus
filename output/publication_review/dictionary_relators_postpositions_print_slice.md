## `kiang`

Category: relator noun

Gloss: beside; near; at the side of

Status: draft-ready with case-boundary caveat

`Kiang` is the clearest first-slice relator noun. It supports a modest dictionary claim for a relational host meaning 'beside / near / at the side of', while keeping explicit that many attested uses appear with case marking and should not be flattened into an independent postposition entry.

(@dict:rel-kiang-1)
a. Tedim: kiangah
b. Segmentation: kiang-ah
c. Gloss: side-LOC
d. Translation: 'at the side'

## Deferred and boundary material

This dictionary slice stays deliberately narrow. `nuai` and `mai` remain boundary material rather than first-slice dictionary entries. `tawhin` remains deferred because the current evidence is sparse and instrument-like. `kipan` and `kipanin` rows involving `ki` remain boundary material.
