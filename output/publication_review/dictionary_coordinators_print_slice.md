# Editorial scope

These are short print-facing dictionary drafts for the coordinators slice. They are aligned to `candidates_coordinators.tsv`, interpreted through `dossier_coordinators.md`, and cross-referenced to `grammar_coordinators_print_slice.md`.

These entries are editorial review material only. They do not imply changes to analyzer dictionaries, machine dictionary files, or lexical source tables. This slice preserves candidate-backed examples and caveats rather than turning the topic into a raw search over every `le`, `leh`, `a`, `mawh`, `ahih hangin`, `ahih kei leh`, `ciangin`, `hangin`, or other coordinator-looking token. Review-note work for coordinators has not yet begun.

## le

Category: NP conjunction

Gloss: 'and'

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "NP coordination with `le`"*

Status: draft-ready

`Le` is the current safe NP-conjunction anchor. The accepted example is Genesis 1:1 `vantung le leitung`. It supports the modest dictionary claim that `le` can join noun phrases, but it does **not** license broad raw `le` harvesting or a wider search over every surface `le` token.

No separate blocked `le` row exists in this first pass. Overgeneration is controlled here by curated selection rather than by a broad blocked-token list.

(@dict:le-coord-1)
a. Tedim: vantung le leitung
b. Segmentation: van-tung | le | lei-tung
c. Gloss: sky-on | and | land-on
d. Translation: 'heaven and earth'

## leh

Category: clause-linking / conditional boundary form

Gloss: 'and; if'; boundary only in this slice

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "`Leh` as conditional or boundary material"*

Status: not draft-ready as simple clause coordinator

The current coordinators packet does not yet have a clean accepted simple clause-conjunction `leh` row. Genesis 13:9 keeps `leh` visible only as conditional or boundary material, so this entry should not be glossed simply as "`and`" without the conditional caveat.

The row also carries an analyzer or export caveat: `kei` is glossed as `NEG` in the selected window even though the wider English context is "I will go." That caveat should stay attached to the row, not reopen pronouns or negation.

(@dict:leh-boundary-1)
a. Tedim: veilam na lak leh kei taklamah ka pai ding hi
b. Segmentation: vei-lam | na | lak | leh | kei | taklam-ah | ka | pai | ding | hi
c. Gloss: faint-direction | 2SG | among | and/if | NEG [export caveat] | right-LOC | 1SG | go | IRR | DECL
d. Translation: 'if thou wilt take the left hand, then I will go to the right'
e. Status: boundary evidence, not print-ready as simple clause conjunction

## a

Category: sequential-linking boundary form / agreement false-friend control

Gloss: exported as `3SG` / `FUNC` in current rows

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Sequential `a` and agreement-`a` false friends"*

Status: not draft-ready as coordinator

`A` is high-risk material and must not be harvested raw. Genesis 2:10 keeps one possible sequential-linkage window visible, but it is not print-ready as a core coordinator example because the analyzer still exports `a` as `3SG` / `FUNC`. This entry therefore functions mainly as a warning or control entry rather than as a positive coordinator entry.

(@dict:a-boundary-1)
a. Tedim: luang a tua mun panin gun hong kikhenin
b. Segmentation: luang | a | tua | mun | pan-in | gun | hong | kikhen-in
c. Gloss: flow | 3SG | that | place | ABL-ERG | river | 3→1 | separate-CVB
d. Translation: 'and from thence it was parted'
e. Status: caveated boundary evidence, not print-ready as coordinator

Blocked control: `a piangsak`

Genesis 1:1 `a piangsak` is blocked as agreement or function material, not coordinator evidence. That control is what keeps raw `a` harvesting from flooding the packet.

## mawh

Category: disjunction / alternative-question candidate

Gloss: 'or' in the generated report; current export not confirmed

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "`Mawh` deferred"*

Status: deferred / not print-ready

The generated report mentions `mawh` as disjunction or alternative-question material. The current candidate layer, however, only has Genesis 6:3 `mawh`, where the export gives lexical or analyzer-noise material glossed as `sin` / `V` rather than as disjunction. `Mawh` therefore remains deferred and not print-ready.

Report-only schematic examples such as `mi mawh ganhing mawh` or `pai ding mawh om ding mawh` should not be printed unless a later analyzer-backed row is actually located.

(@dict:mawh-deferred)
a. Tedim: mawh
b. Segmentation: mawh
c. Gloss: sin [export]
d. Status: deferred, not print-ready as disjunction

## ahih hangin

Category: adversative connector

Gloss: 'but'; with internal-analysis caveat

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Adversative `Ahih hangin`"*

Status: draft-ready with caveat

`Ahih hangin` is the current adversative connector anchor. It is usable with caveat as 'but', but it remains internally analyzable as `ahih` + `hang-in`. This entry should not be used to open a full causal or subordinator chapter.

(@dict:ahih-hangin-1)
a. Tedim: Ahih hangin
b. Segmentation: Ahih | hang-in
c. Gloss: be.3SG.REL | because-ERG
d. Translation: 'but'

## ahih kei leh

Category: conditional-adversative boundary expression

Gloss: 'otherwise; if not'; boundary only

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Conditional-adversative `ahih kei leh`"*

Status: usable only as boundary evidence

`Ahih kei leh` is useful, but it is not a simple coordinator. It overlaps with negation and conditional `leh`, and it should not be flattened into plain "`and`" or used to reopen negation. This slice also does not turn it into a broad dictionary entry for all conditional or adversative material.

(@dict:ahih-kei-leh-1)
a. Tedim: ahih kei leh
b. Segmentation: ahih | kei | leh
c. Gloss: be.3SG.REL | NEG | and/if
d. Translation: 'otherwise' / 'if not'

## Deferred and blocked material

This dictionary slice stays deliberately narrow. Simple clause-conjunction `leh` remains deferred. Full sequential-`a` analysis remains deferred. `Mawh` as disjunction or alternative-question material remains deferred. `A piangsak` remains blocked as agreement or function material. `Ciangin` and broader `hangin` temporal or causal subordinator material remain deferred. Clause-chaining or converb coordination remains deferred. Raw generated-report frequency tables remain excluded. Sentence-final particles remain out of scope.

Broad TAM, directionals, chrestomathy, Mizo/lus, and other Kuki-Chin language work likewise remain deferred. The next step after this dictionary slice is `review_notes_coordinators.md`, but review-note work for coordinators has not yet begun.
