# What the report claims

The dedicated report presents Tedim as a switch-reference system with same-subject `-in`, different-subject material without `-in`, and a common `ahih ciangin` pattern. It also treats clause chaining as an ordinary extension of that system.

# Label decision

The safest grammar-facing label is **same-subject and different-subject clause linkage**, not a full switch-reference system. The secure evidence is same-subject converb linkage; the different-subject-looking material is only support; and `ngenin` is too unstable morphologically to remain promoted.

# Boundary and deferred material

Ordinary `ciangin` 'when' remains temporal subordination. Purposive `dingin` 'in order to / clause-bound irrealis' remains boundary material. Relative-clause material (`a bawl mi`) and nominalization material (`omna`, `muhna-ah`) stay outside the core subject-tracking claim.

`VERB-in` is retained as a report-level comparison term only. It is not enough to rescue `ngenin` as a secure promoted converb in the current evidence.

# Concrete conclusion

The report's switch-reference label is too strong for this packet. The print-facing section should be titled **same-subject and different-subject clause linkage**, with `bawlin` and `semin` promoted, `ahih ciangin` kept as support only, `ngenin` demoted, and `ciangin`, `dingin`, relatives, and nominalization held as boundary material.
