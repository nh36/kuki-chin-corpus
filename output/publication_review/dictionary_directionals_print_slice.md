# Editorial scope

These are short print-facing dictionary drafts for the directionals slice. They are aligned to `candidates_directionals.tsv`, interpreted through `dossier_directionals.md`, and cross-referenced to `grammar_directionals_print_slice.md`.

These entries are editorial review material only. They do not imply changes to analyzer dictionaries, machine dictionary files, or lexical source tables. This slice preserves candidate-backed examples and caveats rather than turning the topic into a raw search over every `khia`, `khiat`, `toh`, `lam`, `sawn`, `lut`, `suk`, `phei`, `cip`, `tang`, or directional-looking token.

## `-khia`

Category: outward directional suffix

Gloss: out; outward

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Outward and away direction"*

Status: draft-ready

`-Khia` is represented by the clean outward anchor `pokhia`. It supports a modest dictionary claim that `-khia` can mark outward motion or direction, but it does **not** license raw `khia` harvesting or every orthographic `khia` sequence.

(@dict:dir-khia-1)
a. Tedim: pokhia
b. Segmentation: po-khia
c. Gloss: grow-out
d. Translation: 'grew'

## `-toh`

Category: upward directional suffix / comitative-overlap form

Gloss: up; with comitative/accompany caveat

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Upward direction and directionals in the verb phrase"*

Status: usable only with overlap caveat

`Kilaktoh` is the current upward `-toh` anchor, but `-toh` is polysemous and must not be glossed simply as UP in all contexts. The dictionary entry therefore pairs upward evidence with the explicit `paitoh` caveat rather than flattening all `-toh` rows into one directional meaning.

(@dict:dir-toh-1)
a. Tedim: kilaktoh
b. Segmentation: ki-lak-toh
c. Gloss: REFL-take-UP
d. Translation: 'was taken up'

Blocked control: `paitoh`

`Paitoh` is `go-accompany` / comitative-overlap material, not upward-directional evidence. It should remain the false-friend control that keeps `-toh` from being printed as simply upward in every context.

## Deferred and blocked material

This dictionary slice stays deliberately narrow. Raw generated-report counts remain excluded. Raw suffix harvesting remains excluded. `Paitoh` remains blocked as comitative/accompany material. Nominalized `-na` forms are not identical to finite directional predicates. `-lut`, `-phei`, `-cip`, and `-tang` remain deferred or not print-ready. Broad VP-slot or TAM prose remains out of scope. Chrestomathy, Mizo/lus, and other Kuki-Chin language work remain out of scope.

The next step after this dictionary slice is `output/publication_review/review_notes_directionals.md`, but review-note work has not yet begun.
