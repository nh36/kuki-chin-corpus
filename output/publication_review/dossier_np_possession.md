# NP structure / possession dossier

## Scope and status

This dossier is the normalized interpretation layer for the NP structure / possession section. It reads `candidates_np_possession.tsv`, the two report chapters, and the checked supplement `examples_np_possession_normalization.tsv` together so the publication-facing print slice can make controlled claims without promoting raw report counts.

The section remains candidate-controlled. It is no longer just a scoping note, but it still does not claim a full Tedim NP template or a complete possession theory.

## Safe publication-facing claims

1. `hih mite` is still the clearest demonstrative-related NP anchor and safely supports a demonstrative-before-noun claim.
2. `mi khat`, `ni li`, `kum nih`, and `kum sawm le nih` show that noun-plus-postnominal numeral structure is real, while the full numeral analysis stays in the numerals section.
3. `mi khempeuh`, `mi pawlkhat`, and `mi tampi` support a modest noun-plus-quantifier claim, while the fuller semantics stay in the quantifiers section.
4. `na pa' inn-ah` and `a zi' min` are safe enough for a cautious possession subsection, supporting possessor-before-possessed structure without claiming a full possession paradigm.

## Safe only with explicit caveats

- `mi khat` remains on the numeral versus indefinite boundary.
- `mi pawlkhat` and `mi tampi` are useful NP-order rows, but their semantics are broader than simple quantifier labels.
- `na pa' inn-ah`, `ka pa`, and `Topa' inn` show why possession belongs in the section, but they still overlap with possessive prefixes, apostrophe or genitive analysis, and case or relator closure.
- Gospel noun-plus-numeral and noun-plus-quantifier examples are clean enough to print, but no equally clean Gospel possession row was found in the current pass.

## Deferred and boundary material

- A full possession paradigm is deferred.
- Kinship possession beyond a few anchors is deferred.
- `a pa' inn` remains too boundary-heavy for a core printed possession example because it stacks prefix possession and apostrophe-marked possession.
- `Topa' tungah` remains a relator or case-boundary row rather than a core possession anchor.
- `ka suahna leitang` remains a nominalization boundary row.
- Isolated prefix surfaces, analyzer-noisy rows, and broad recursive-possession claims remain out of print for now.

## Current editorial conclusion

The normalized section can now present NP structure and possession together as a coherent publication-facing chapter fragment. The secure core is basic NP ordering plus a cautious possession subsection. Broader claims about determiner order, genitive marking, possessive paradigms, and the full interaction with pronouns, relators, and case marking remain explicitly deferred.
