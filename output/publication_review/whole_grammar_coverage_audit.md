# Audit scope

This is an architecture-level audit and planning note, not a new grammar packet and not a printable grammar bundle. It is controlled by `docs/grammar/grammar_source_map.json`, `docs/grammar/GRAMMAR_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md`, `docs/SKELETON_GRAMMAR.md`, `output/grammar/grammar_full.md`, `output/publication_review/remaining_retrofit_inventory.md`, and `output/publication_review/human_review_handoff.md`.

The current publication-review packet sequence is complete for the packets already represented in `output/publication_review/`, but that does **not** mean the whole Tedim grammar is complete at the same maturity level.

# 1. Grammar architecture

The project’s Tedim grammar architecture has several layers, and they do different jobs:

1. `docs/SKELETON_GRAMMAR.md` is the chapter scaffold. It is the broadest whole-grammar outline, spanning phonology, nominal morphology, pronominal systems, verbal morphology, sentence-final material, nominalization, and compounds.
2. `docs/grammar/grammar_source_map.json` is the canonical Tedim topic/construction layer for integrated grammar routing. It is the authoritative machine-readable map for topic-level integration, even though it does **not** by itself prove that every chapter-scale domain has already been lifted into publication-review packets.
3. `docs/grammar/GRAMMAR_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md` is the human-readable map of reports, literature reviews, morpheme files, generated outputs, and analyzer-gap documents. It is the best repository-level source for seeing which grammar domains have evidence but no packet yet.
4. `output/publication_review/` is the candidate-first review layer. A domain reaches the current slice maturity level when it has the aligned packet surfaces appropriate to its packet type: candidate TSV, dossier, grammar print slice, review notes, and tests, plus a dictionary print slice when the packet is lexical or entry-based. Constructional packets such as VP structure / suffix stacking may stop short of an ordinary dictionary slice when the safe claim is about ordering or construction rather than about a headword entry. Some narrow lexical-morphology packets may also defer a dictionary layer when lexical treatment would overclaim an unresolved editorial split; the current derivation / valency `-sak` packet is in that narrower state.
5. `output/grammar/grammar_full.md` is a drafting/integration output, not proof of completeness. It is generated from the source map and backend integration layer, and it remains useful for spotting routing gaps or thin sections rather than for claiming that the whole Tedim grammar is editorially complete.

`output/publication_review/remaining_retrofit_inventory.md` is therefore useful but too narrow to stand in for the whole architecture by itself. It tracks the recent publication-review retrofit sequence, not the entire grammar scaffold represented by the skeleton grammar, source inventory, and generated full grammar.
