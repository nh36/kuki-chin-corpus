# Editorial scope

These are short print-facing dictionary drafts for the TAM / aspect / modal slice. They are aligned to `candidates_tam.tsv`, interpreted through `dossier_tam_scope.md`, and cross-referenced to `grammar_tam_print_slice.md`.

These entries are editorial review material only. They do not imply changes to analyzer dictionaries, machine dictionary files, or lexical source tables. This is a dictionary draft layer, not a machine-dictionary edit and not a full tam chapter. The slice preserves candidate-backed examples and caveats rather than turning TAM into a raw search over every TAM-looking suffix, stack, or clause-bound form. Review-note work for TAM has not yet begun.

## Deferred and blocked material

This dictionary slice stays deliberately narrow. `pailai` / `-lai` is not dictionary-ready because of lexical `lai` / `go-midst` overlap. `dingin` and other clause-bound `-ding` material are not dictionary-ready. `khiathei ding om lo` and other negative modal stacks remain overlap controls, not dictionary anchors. `mangngilh ta hi` and other sentence-final overlap rows are not dictionary-ready TAM evidence. `khia-ta` and directional/TAM stacking remain deferred. `bawlzoding` and `bawlsakthei` remain broader VP-slot stacks rather than compact dictionary anchors. `-nawn` and `-khin` remain deferred until cleaner anchors are selected.

The next step after this dictionary slice is `review_notes_tam.md`, but review-note work for TAM has not yet begun.
