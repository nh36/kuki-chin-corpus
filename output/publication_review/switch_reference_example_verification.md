## Display verdict

`bawlin` stays promoted, but the printed excerpt should be shortened so the target `bawl-in` is visible without the full verse crowding it out.

`semin` stays promoted. The Deuteronomy example is compact enough to keep in full, and `beel-in` should remain boundary material rather than a second converb-like anchor. The `na` gloss stays `2SG` to match the prefix/agreement slice.

`ahih ciangin` stays support only and should not be promoted to a dedicated different-subject marker.

`ngenin` stays blocked because the morphology is still not secure enough.

## Boundary-only checks

Ordinary `ciangin` and purposive `dingin` stay boundary material. Relative-clause `a bawl mi` and nominalization material `omna` and `muhna-ah` also stay boundary-controlled.
