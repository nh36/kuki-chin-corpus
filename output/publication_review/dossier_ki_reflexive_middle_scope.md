# Safest first grammar-facing lift

The first grammar-facing lift should center on:

1. Reciprocal `ki-gawm` as the clearest core anchor.
2. Subject-affected `ki-lak-toh` as controlled middle-like support.
3. Cautious passive-like `kigen` treatment without collapsing into a solved passive system.

Everything else stays explicitly boundary-controlled.
