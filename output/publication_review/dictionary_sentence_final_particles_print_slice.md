# Editorial scope

These are short print-facing dictionary drafts for the sentence-final particles slice. They are aligned to `candidates_sentence_final_particles.tsv`, interpreted through `dossier_sentence_final_particles.md`, and cross-referenced to `grammar_sentence_final_particles_print_slice.md`.

These entries are editorial review material only. They do not imply changes to analyzer dictionaries, machine dictionary files, or lexical source tables. This slice preserves candidate-backed examples and caveats rather than turning the topic into a raw search over every `hi`, `hiam`, `in`, `un`, `tahen`, `hen`, `aw`, `ta`, `zo`, or other particle-looking token. Review-note work for sentence-final particles has not yet begun.

## hi

Category: declarative particle / copula-plus-declarative overlap form

Gloss: `DECL`; declarative marker, only construction-controlled in this slice

Cross-reference: *Grammar review slice, "Declarative `hi` with copula overlap"; "Negative-plus-declarative `lo hi`"*

Status: draft-ready only with caveat

`Hi` is visible in the current packet through `ahi hi` and `thusim lo hi`. `Ahi hi` is copula-plus-declarative evidence, not bare `hi` evidence. `Thusim lo hi` is negation-overlap evidence and overlaps the stabilized negation packet. Raw `hi` hits should not be treated as sentence-final declarative evidence, and this slice should not reopen negation or copula analysis.

(@dict:sfp-hi-ahi-hi)
a. Tedim: ahi hi
b. Segmentation: ahi | hi
c. Gloss: be.3SG | DECL
d. Translation: 'it was'

(@dict:sfp-hi-lo-hi)

b. Segmentation: thusim | lo | hi
c. Gloss: parable | NEG | DECL
d. Translation: 'he had not respect' / 'it was not accepted'

Directionals, chrestomathy, Mizo/lus, and other Kuki-Chin languages remain out of scope.
