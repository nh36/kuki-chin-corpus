## Recommendation

Demonstratives, negation, pronouns / clusivity, stem alternation, case marking, interrogatives, numerals, quantifiers, coordinators, sentence-final particles, and directionals now all have publication-review packets at the current candidate-first level.

Quantifiers now has a first analyzer-aware candidate TSV plus curated extractor support, a first candidate-controlled dossier, a first grammar print slice, a first dictionary print slice, and review notes, so the quantifiers packet is now ready for human review at the current slice maturity level. Coordinators now has `candidates_coordinators.tsv`, a curated extractor route, `dossier_coordinators.md`, `grammar_coordinators_print_slice.md`, `dictionary_coordinators_print_slice.md`, and `review_notes_coordinators.md`, so the coordinators packet is also ready for human review at the current slice maturity level. Sentence-final particles now has `candidates_sentence_final_particles.tsv`, curated extractor support, `dossier_sentence_final_particles.md`, `grammar_sentence_final_particles_print_slice.md`, `dictionary_sentence_final_particles_print_slice.md`, and `review_notes_sentence_final_particles.md`, so the sentence-final particles packet is now ready for human review at the current slice maturity level. A compact reviewer-orientation handoff now exists at `output/publication_review/human_review_handoff.md`, and a final packet-integrity check now exists at `tests/test_publication_review_packet_integrity.py`. Directionals now has `candidates_directionals.tsv`, a curated extractor route, `dossier_directionals.md`, `grammar_directionals_print_slice.md`, `dictionary_directionals_print_slice.md`, and `review_notes_directionals.md`, so the directionals packet is also ready for human review at the current slice maturity level. Broad TAM / aspect / modal now has an initial candidate TSV plus scoping dossier at `candidates_tam.tsv` and `dossier_tam_scope.md`; the safest next TAM step is a compact grammar slice limited to simple suffixal anchors such as `-ngei`, `-gige`, `-zel`, `-ta`, `-zo`, `-kik`, `-ding`, and `-thei`, while `-lai`, `dingin`, negation overlap, sentence-final overlap, and TAM stacking remain deferred. Chrestomathy, Mizo/lus, and other non-Tedim or non-slice work should remain deferred until they are chosen intentionally as a new scope.
